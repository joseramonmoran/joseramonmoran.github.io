Instructor

Industrial Organization (ECO-14301)
Instituto Tecnológico Autónomo de México, Winter 2026

Undergraduate course on firm behavior under imperfect competition: market structure and concentration, pricing and product differentiation, entry and entry deterrence, vertical relationships, and the empirical methods used to estimate demand and measure market power. The course closes with applications to competition policy and regulation.
